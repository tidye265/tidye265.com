Getting Started & Account

To join tiDye265, click on the "Register" or "Me" icon. Enter your active Malawian mobile number, create a secure password, and confirm that you are over 18 years old. Once submitted, your account is ready instantly.
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your registered phone number, and we will send you an OTP (One Time Password) to reset it securely.

Deposits & Withdrawals

Go to your "Me" (Account) page and select "Deposit". Choose your preferred mobile money network (Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba), enter the amount, and follow the prompt on your phone to enter your PIN.
Deposit Limits: Minimum MWK 200 – Maximum MWK 750,000 per transaction.
Deposits reflect in your tiDye265 wallet instantly.
We pride ourselves on fast payouts. Standard withdrawals are processed automatically and usually reflect in your mobile money account within 1 to 5 minutes. Large withdrawals may undergo a brief security check.

Gameplay & Betting Rules

In P2P games, you are playing directly against other users on the 265 network, not the house. tiDye265 provides the secure platform and ensures the funds are locked safely until the game finishes and the winner is declared.
If an event is cancelled, postponed, or abandoned, the bet will be declared "Void" according to our official rules, and your stake will be fully refunded to your wallet.
